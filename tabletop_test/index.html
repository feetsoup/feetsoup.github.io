Players

Rename stats, use − and + to change values. “Add piece” drops a token for that player on the current canvas.

Items
Stat modifiers (apply while the item is in a player’s inventory)
Drag items onto the table or into a player’s inventory, and back here to put them away. Click an item to edit it.

Tiles

Draw on the table (Edit mode)

Drag to move, drag the corner to resize, scroll to zoom, drag empty table to pan.

Math examples: x*2, x-3, (x+1)/2, x^2. A function tile ignores its shortcut.

Options

Silences the piano, music player and the teddy bear.

Scales the top bar, menus and results box. Zoom the table itself by scrolling.

Play together

Host a game and share its code, or join someone else’s game with theirs. Everyone sees each other’s mouse on the table.

Everyone plays on the host’s tables and sees every change live. The host decides what each guest may do. When a guest leaves, their own tables come back exactly as they were.

Save and load

Files are saved to your computer as .json and include the images you imported.

Save

“This table” saves the current table plus the players, items and images it uses. “All tables” saves everything: every table, all players, items, imported images and tool settings.

Load

“Load table(s)” adds the tables in a file to what you have now. “Load all” replaces everything with the contents of an all-tables file.
